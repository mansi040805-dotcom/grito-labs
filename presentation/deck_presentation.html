Algorithmic Rigor

Markov Chain Transition Matrices & Removal Effects

Mathematical Formulation

Rather than imposing arbitrary rules, Markov Chains model customer journeys as a state transition graph:

P(i, j) = C(i, j) / Σ_k C(i, k)
P(Conv) = Σ_paths ∏ P(s_t, s_{t+1})
RE(c) = [P(Conv) - P(Conv \ {c})] / P(Conv)
W_Markov(c) = RE(c) / Σ_k RE(k)

A channel's credit is proportional to the systemic shock that would occur if that channel were eliminated from Grito Labs' marketing ecosystem.
