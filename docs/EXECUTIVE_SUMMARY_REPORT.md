### Key Distortion Insights:
1. **Google Search is Artificially Inflated (+48.2% LTA Bias):** Under Last-Touch, Google Search receives credit for 496 enrollments. Under Markov Chain modeling, its true algorithmic contribution is **334.7 enrollments**. The extra 161.3 conversions represent prospective students who entered the funnel through YouTube, Meta, or Organic channels and merely clicked a branded Google Search link to navigate back to the checkout page.

### 4.1 Google Search (Spend: \$82,007.20 | Markov ROAS: 4.82x | CAC: \$245.03)
* **Strategic Role:** Primary high-intent demand harvester.
* **Findings:** The brand search component (`GS_Brand_Defense`) generates a 10.42x ROAS with a \$1.25 CPC, serving as essential defense against competitor bidding. However, `GS_Competitor_Conquest` suffers from high CPCs (\$4.10) and low conversion velocity (1.92x ROAS).
* **Action:** Maintain overall investment; trim conquesting and reinvest in high-intent analytics bootcamp search queries.

### 4.2 Meta Ads (Spend: \$85,764.16 | Markov ROAS: 4.05x | CAC: \$291.76)
* **Strategic Role:** Primary top-of-funnel discovery engine.
* **Findings:** Meta generated **2,632,597 impressions** and originated 449 converted journeys. Career-switcher video testimonials and Reels demonstrating real Root Cause Analysis (RCA) capstones drove exceptional engagement.
* **Action:** Rebalance creative mix toward Reels; maintain budget baseline.

### 4.3 YouTube Ads (Spend: \$42,456.05 | Markov ROAS: 4.24x | CAC: \$278.49)
* **Strategic Role:** Educational lead magnet and curriculum trust builder.
* **Findings:** Pre-roll ads offering free 2-hour SQL crash courses generated high-quality leads that converted at 37.2% when nurtured via email.
* **Action:** **Scale spend by +25%** (add \$10.6k); YouTube has substantial unexhausted headroom before hitting audience saturation.

### 4.4 Email Marketing (Spend: \$5,218.13 | Markov ROAS: 47.98x | CAC: \$24.61)
* **Strategic Role:** Mid-funnel conversion accelerator.
* **Findings:** Lowest cost, highest leverage channel. Drives 212.0 conversions at \$24.61 CAC. Drip sequences promoting Sunday Masterclasses convert warm prospects who hesitated on first visit.
* **Action:** **Scale spend by +50%** (add \$2.6k) to expand automated drip workflows and webinar hosting infrastructure.

### 4.5 LinkedIn Ads (Spend: \$47,451.14 | Markov ROAS: 2.16x | CAC: \$547.62)
* **Strategic Role:** Working professional upskilling.
* **Findings:** **Severe capital underperformance.** Steep CPCs (\$7.40) and low CTRs (1.3%) resulted in a \$547.62 CAC—more than double Google Search. Absorbed 18% of portfolio spend but contributed only 6.2% of conversions.
* **Action:** **Slash spend by -40%** (reclaim \$19.0k); restrict targeting strictly to warm retargeting for enterprise fellowship applicants.

## 9. Tracking Privacy, Data Hygiene & Incrementality Governance

* Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and browser cookie lifetime caps obscure ~18% of mobile web touchpoints.
* **Architecture Solution:** Grito Labs must complete the deployment of server-to-server **Meta Conversions API (CAPI)** and **Google Enhanced Conversions**. By passing hashed emails and phone numbers captured during syllabus downloads, Grito Labs achieves **94.2% identity resolution** across devices.

### 9.2 Quasi-Experimental Incrementality (Geo-Holdouts)
* Attribution models identify correlation; incrementality testing proves causation.
* We recommend conducting a 30-day regional geo-matched holdout test on Google Brand Search (`GS_Brand_Defense`) across three tier-2 cities. Pausing brand search in test regions will quantify the exact percentage of organic search cannibalization.
